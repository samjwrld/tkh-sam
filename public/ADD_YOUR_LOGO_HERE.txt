===================================================
THE KONCEPT HOUSE - LOGO ATTACHMENT INSTRUCTIONS
===================================================

To upload or attach your custom brand logo:

1. Save your logo image as `logo.png` or `logo.svg`.
2. Upload or copy your file into this `/public` folder.
3. If using `logo.png`, place it directly at `/public/logo.png`.
4. If using `logo.svg`, replace the placeholder `/public/logo.svg`.

Dimensions Recommended:
- 200x200 px or 500x500 px (Square 1:1 ratio)
- Transparent background PNG or SVG.
